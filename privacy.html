Privacy notice

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Pocket Crayon Workshop Inc. (“PCW”, “we” or “us”) is a New Brunswick, Canada–based company. This notice explains how we handle personal information when you visit pocketcrayonworkshop.ca or contact us about the company.

Scope: This notice covers the PCW corporate website and enquiries sent to PCW. It does not govern personal information collected within Crew Closet or other individual apps. Our products have their own privacy notices where applicable; please review the relevant product notice when using an app.

Information we receive

Our corporate website is informational. It does not have user accounts, a contact form, or a checkout. If you email us, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, message and any attachments. You do not need to send sensitive personal information to make a general enquiry.

When you visit the site, the services that deliver it may process technical information, such as your IP address, browser information, requested pages and access times, for website delivery, security and troubleshooting.

How we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries, communicate about our company or products when appropriate, manage business correspondence, maintain website security and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not currently send marketing emails or subscribe people to a mailing list when they contact us.

Service providers and other websites

We use Northmail for our corporate email and GitHub Pages to publish this website. Those providers may process information needed to provide their services. Our site also displays Crew Closet artwork loaded from the Crew Closet website, which may receive technical request information when your browser loads that image. Service providers may process information in other jurisdictions, where different laws may apply.

Our site links to external product websites, including Crew Closet. When you follow those links, the destination's own privacy practices apply. We do not sell personal information submitted through the corporate website.

Cookies and analytics

The PCW corporate website does not currently use an advertising tracker or a dedicated visitor analytics script. The website's hosting and linked resources may still process technical request data required to deliver their services. If we add analytics, advertising technology or other personal-information collection in the future, we will update this notice and take any necessary consent steps.

Retention and protection

We keep correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to handle your enquiry, maintain appropriate business records or meet legal obligations, and then delete or securely dispose of it when no longer required. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the information involved. No email or internet service is completely risk-free.

Your privacy questions and requests

You may contact us to ask what personal information we hold about you, request access or correction, or raise a privacy concern. We will review requests and respond in accordance with applicable law. You may also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada if you have an unresolved concern.

Changes to this notice
